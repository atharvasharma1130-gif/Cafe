Our Story

DaFam is a quiet, warmly lit café built around honest food and generous tables. From hand-stretched pizzas to comforting pastas, every plate is made to be shared and remembered.

The Signature

Neapolitan pizza,

Slow-fermented dough, a blistered airy crust, sweet tomato and creamy burrata finished with rocket and basil oil. Widely loved as one of the best places for Neapolitan pizza in Meerut.
